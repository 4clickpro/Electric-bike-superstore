<h3 style="color:var(--accent,#c9a94e)">Quick Answer</h3>
    <p><strong>The five best golf cart upgrades for 2026 are a lithium battery conversion, LED lighting, Bluetooth audio, upgraded seating, and a suspension or lift kit</strong>. Lithium batteries deliver the biggest payoff — more range, faster charging, and double the lifespan — while lighting and audio are quick wins that change how your cart feels every ride. Prefer to skip the work? Rent a brand new cart from us instead: our 6-seat and 8-seat rentals already come with Bluetooth audio and premium seating, from $99/day.</p>

    <h3>1. Lithium Battery Conversion</h3>
    <p>The single highest-impact upgrade. Lithium batteries weigh a fraction of lead-acid, charge in a fraction of the time, deliver more usable range, and typically last about twice as long. The upfront cost is real, but for owners who use their cart daily, it pays for itself in fewer replacements and better performance.</p>

    <h3>2. LED Lighting</h3>
    <p>Swap outdated bulbs for LED headlights, taillights, and underglow. LEDs are brighter, draw less power, and last for years. They also make your cart look sharp — and street-legal lighting is a must if you drive on roads like the ones in Destin and Miramar Beach.</p>

    <h3>3. Bluetooth Audio</h3>
    <p>Marine-grade Bluetooth speakers and sound bars are the most popular lifestyle upgrade. They're weather-resistant, easy to install, and turn every ride into a beach party. Many new carts, including our 6-seat Land Rover style rentals, include Bluetooth audio from the factory.</p>

    <h3>4. Upgraded Seating</h3>
    <p>Premium vinyl seats, rear-facing seats, and flip-down seats transform comfort and capacity. After a long day of cruising, good seats make all the difference — and they're an easy way to add seats for friends and family.</p>

    <h3>5. Suspension and Lift Kits</h3>
    <p>A lift kit with upgraded shocks and all-terrain tires lets your cart handle grass, gravel, and sandy lots with confidence. If you plan to explore beyond paved roads, this is the upgrade that opens up the map.</p>
