    <h3>Clubs: Complete Sets Make It Easy</h3>
    <p>You don't need to piece together a bag. Complete sets include driver, fairway woods, hybrids, irons, wedges, putter, and a stand bag — everything you need in one box. See our <a href="reviews.html">hands-on golf cart and gear reviews</a> for products we've tested.</p>

    <h3>Balls: Bring More Than You Think</h3>
    <p>Plan on <strong>9–12 balls per round</strong>, more if you play near water or thick rough. Premium balls feel great, but mid-range options offer the best value for most golfers.</p>

    <h3>Gloves, Tees, and the Small Stuff</h3>
    <p>A quality glove improves grip and prevents blisters. Carry a pocketful of tees, plus a divot tool and ball marker for the green. These small items cost little and make every round smoother.</p>

    <h3>Bags: Stand Bags for Walkers, Cart Bags for Riders</h3>
    <p>A stand bag is the most versatile choice — it stands on its own at the range and fits on a cart. If you ride most rounds, a cart bag with full-length dividers keeps clubs organized and protected.</p>

    <h3>Comfort Gear</h3>
    <p>Golf shoes with soft spikes, moisture-wicking shirts, and a rain layer round out the kit. Sunscreen and a hat are non-negotiable on the Emerald Coast, where the Florida sun is strong most of the year.</p>
