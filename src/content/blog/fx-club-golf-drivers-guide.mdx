<p><strong>Off the tee, the driver sets up every hole. Yet it is the club most amateurs struggle with — usually because the driver does not match their swing. This guide explains loft, shaft flex, and forgiveness so your next driver works with you, not against you.</strong></p>

    <h3>Higher Loft, Higher Launch</h3>
    <p>Beginner and slower-swing players should look at 10.5°–12° loft. More loft means less spin, more carry, and far fewer weak slices.</p>

    <h3>Forgiveness Comes From the Head</h3>
    <p>Larger heads (440–460cc) with perimeter weighting keep the ball straighter on off-center hits. Draw-bias settings help players who tend to slice.</p>

    <h3>Match the Shaft to Your Speed</h3>
    <p>Swing speed determines flex: seniors and many women use senior/ladies flex, average players regular, and fast swingers stiff. The right flex adds distance and control.</p>
