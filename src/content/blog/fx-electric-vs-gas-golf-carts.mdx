<h3 style="color:var(--accent,#c9a94e)">Quick Answer</h3>
    <p><strong>For most people, electric golf carts are the better choice: they cost less to run and maintain, run quietly, and produce zero fumes</strong>. Gas carts still make sense for very large properties, long daily distances, or steep terrain. On a Destin or Miramar Beach vacation, electric is a clear winner — our fleet is 100% electric, and rentals start at just $99/day.</p>

    <h3>Running Costs</h3>
    <p>Electric carts cost pennies per mile to charge. Gas carts need regular fill-ups and their fuel economy is measured in miles per gallon, not miles per kilowatt-hour — over a season of daily use, electric typically saves hundreds of dollars. Battery replacement is the big-ticket item on an electric cart, but it happens every few years, not every month.</p>

    <h3>Maintenance</h3>
    <p>Electric carts have far fewer moving parts: no engine, no oil changes, no air filters, no fuel system to winterize. Gas carts demand oil changes, filter swaps, and occasional carburetor attention. For owners who don't love wrenching, electric wins easily.</p>

    <h3>Performance and Range</h3>
    <p>Modern electric carts cover <strong>25–45 miles per charge</strong> — more than enough for a day of cruising Destin and Miramar Beach — and deliver instant torque for a responsive ride. Gas carts offer quick refueling and strong hill-climbing, which matters mainly on large or mountainous properties.</p>

    <h3>Noise, Fumes, and the Environment</h3>
    <p>Electric carts are quiet enough to carry on a conversation at full speed, produce no exhaust, and are gentle on the environment — a real plus on the beach. Gas carts are louder and smell like fuel. On vacation, electric keeps the vibe relaxed and the air clean.</p>
