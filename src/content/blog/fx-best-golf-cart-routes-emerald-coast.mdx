    <h3>Route 1: Scenic Gulf Drive, Miramar Beach</h3>
    <p>The classic Emerald Coast cruise. Scenic Gulf Drive hugs the beach with restaurants, ice cream shops, and beach accesses every few blocks. Park the cart in dedicated cart parking, walk the sand, then continue toward Destin.</p>

    <h3>Route 3: Destin Harbor Boardwalk</h3>
    <p>Cruise to the Destin Harbor Boardwalk for waterfront dining, boat-watching, and evening lights. Golf cart parking is plentiful along the harbor, and it's an easy ride from any Destin rental property.</p>

    <p>Crab Island itself is boat-only, but carts are perfect for the short drive to the marinas and boat ramps where shuttles leave — then cruise the waterfront restaurants on the way back.</p>

    <h3>Route 5: Grand Boulevard &amp; Grand Boulevard Town Center</h3>
    <p>Between Destin and Miramar Beach, Grand Boulevard's shops, restaurants, and weekly events are an easy, family-friendly cart destination with plenty of parking.</p>
